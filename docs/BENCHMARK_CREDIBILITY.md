## Executive Summary & Audit Overview

To ensure total mathematical defensibility and withstand aggressive judge scrutiny during hackathon evaluation, the benchmark evaluation suite for the **Razorpay AI Revenue Recovery Agent** underwent a comprehensive audit and redesign.

### Previous Evaluation Flaws Identified & Remediated

1. **Elimination of Target Leakage (Circular Ground-Truth Assumptions)**:
   - *Previous Flaw*: Ground truth recoverability (`is_truly_recoverable`) was derived directly from the ML model's prediction (`prediction.recovery_probability >= 0.35`). This represented circular reasoning where the model evaluated itself.
   - *Remediation*: Ground-truth recovery probability $P_{\text{true}}(\text{recovered} \mid X, \text{action}_k)$ is now generated by an independent, unobserved environment simulator (`StochasticRecoveryEnvironment`). Neither the ML probability model nor the decision agent has access to $P_{\text{true}}$ or future random draws.

2. **Fair & Realistic Baseline Implementations**:
   - *Previous Flaw*: Naive retry baselines assumed static 30% recovery rates for all retry actions, ignoring failure categories like checkout abandonment, overdue receivables, or immediate card decline lockouts.
   - *Remediation*: Implemented a realistic 3-Tier Baseline Evaluation Suite:
     - **Baseline 1 (Naive Fixed Retry)**: Immediate retry for payment and subscription declines. Fails 80%+ for insufficient funds because bank accounts do not reload immediately. Produces 0% recovery for checkout abandonment and overdue invoices.
     - **Baseline 2 (Standard Rule-Based Policy)**: Industry standard rule engine (48h retry for insufficient funds, 1h for temporary processing timeouts, update card notification for expired cards).
     - **AI Revenue Recovery Agent**: Candidate Optimization Matrix (9 actions) + ML Recovery Model + Deterministic Safety Guardrails.

3. **Stochastic Noise & Customer Fatigue Dynamics**:
   - *Previous Flaw*: Interventions produced deterministic outcomes without noise or friction.
   - *Remediation*: Introduced a stochastic outcome generator $\text{Outcome} \sim \text{Bernoulli}(P_{\text{true}}(\text{recovered} \mid \text{action}_{k^*}))$ with customer fatigue attenuation ($0.85^{\text{previous\_interventions}}$) and $\pm 5\%$ environmental noise.

## Single-Event Financial Attribution Rules

To prevent double-counting or inflated financial claims:
1. **Single-Event Credit**: Every recovered payment is attributed exactly once to the single intervention $k^*$ that produced the successful Bernoulli draw.
2. **Strict Counterfactual Ledger**: 
   $$\text{Incremental Revenue} = \sum_{i \in \text{Recovered}_{\text{AI}}} \text{Amount}_i - \sum_{j \in \text{Recovered}_{\text{Rule}}} \text{Amount}_j$$
3. **Separation of Expected vs Executed Recovery**:
   - **Expected Value**: $\text{EV} = \text{Amount} \times P_{\text{predicted}}$ (Used purely for internal candidate ranking).
   - **Actual Realized Value**: $\text{AV} = \text{Amount} \times Y_{\text{Bernoulli}}$ (Used for all benchmark statistics and financial claims).
